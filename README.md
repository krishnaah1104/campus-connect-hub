# ScaleX

ScaleX is a college-wide social and coordination platform built to make it easier for students to discover people, start conversations, find the right seniors, and stay connected with campus activities.

#  Problem Statement

Many students in college struggle with two simple problems:

- Starting conversations: Students often find it difficult to interact with people outside their usual circle. Even after spending a year together, many barely know each other unless academics or college activities bring them together.
- Finding the right seniors: Freshers and juniors often want guidance from seniors with specific skills or experience, but they don't know who those people are or how to find them.

#  Solution

ScaleX brings students and instructors onto a single platform with structured profiles and people discovery.

During onboarding, users provide details such as:

- Year and course
- Degree/program
- Skills and interests based filters
- Achievements
- College positions or titles

These details make people discoverable through filters, allowing students to find and directly connect with someone who matches what they are looking for.

# Key Features

#  👥 People Discovery

Find students and seniors based on skills, interests, achievements, academic details, and college roles.

#  🕶️ Anonymous Real-Time Chat

Students can randomly connect and start conversations without immediately revealing their identity.

This reduces the hesitation and fear of being judged, making it easier to interact before meeting IRL.

#  💬 Automatic Groups

Groups are automatically assigned based on onboarding data.

For example, a student selecting UniVel 2 during onboarding is automatically added to the UniVel 2 group.

Users can also access the complete group history, including messages sent before they joined.

#  📢 Campus Announcements

TAs, student council members, club members, instructors, and other authorized users can post campus-related announcements with relevant tags.

Users can filter announcements by tags to quickly find the updates that matter to them.

How It Connects

The main idea is to keep everything connected:

Onboarding → People Discovery → Anonymous Chat → Groups → Announcements

Instead of using different platforms for different purposes, ScaleX brings college-wide interaction and coordination into one place while keeping personal conversations separate.

Tech & Build

ScaleX is currently built as a web application, with the goal of making it an installable PWA.

The platform uses real-time communication, structured user profiles, filtering, automatic group assignment, and role-based announcement publishing to create a more organized college community.
